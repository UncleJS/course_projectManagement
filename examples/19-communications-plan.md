## Communication Principles

The following principles govern all communications on the Meridian project:

1. **Written record**: All material project decisions and commitments are documented and retained in the project file. Verbal agreements are followed up in writing within 24 hours.
2. **No surprises**: Stakeholders are never surprised by significant news. They hear it from the project team before they hear it elsewhere.
3. **Bad news travels up immediately**: Emerging issues, risks materializing, or deviations from plan are escalated to the Sponsor on the same day — not at the next scheduled report.
4. **Proportionate frequency**: Stakeholders receive communication at a frequency that serves their needs — not more, not less. The project team is not the city's newsletter service.
5. **Two-way**: Communication is not broadcasting. Feedback is actively sought, recorded, and acted upon. Stakeholders who raise concerns receive a response.

## Communication Channels

| Channel | Purpose | Who can use | Retention required? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email (city email system) | Formal communications, document distribution, decision records | All | Yes — project file (SharePoint) |
| MS Teams | Day-to-day team collaboration, stand-up calls, supplier calls | Project team + GovTech | Yes — key decisions extracted and archived in project file |
| SharePoint (NDA project site) | Document repository, version control, project file | Project team + Sponsor | Yes — all project documents |
| GovTech project portal | Build tracking, sprint backlog, defect log (during build phase) | Project team + GovTech | Yes — GovTech retains; PM exports at close |
| In-person meetings | Gate reviews, Project Board, key stakeholder briefings | As scheduled | Yes — minutes required |
| Phone / video call | Urgent issues, informal updates | All | Yes — follow-up email to confirm decisions |

**Note**: Personal messaging apps (WhatsApp, iMessage) are **not** approved channels for project decisions or sensitive information. Any decision made informally must be confirmed by email.
