## Communication Principles

*State the 3–5 principles that will govern communications on this project. Examples:*

1. All material project communications are written and retained in the project file.
2. Bad news travels up immediately; good news travels in the normal reporting cycle.
3. Stakeholders are never surprised — they hear significant news from the project team before they hear it elsewhere.
4. Meeting time is protected; every meeting has an agenda and produces minutes with actions.
5. Communication is two-way — feedback is actively sought and acted upon.

## Escalation Communication Protocol

| Situation | Who communicates | To whom | Within | Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Risk becoming an issue | PM | Sponsor | 24 hours | Email + phone |
| Budget overrun forecast >5% | PM | Sponsor + Finance | 24 hours | Exception report |
| Schedule slip >2 weeks (critical path) | PM | Sponsor | 24 hours | Exception report |
| Contractor performance failure | PM | Sponsor + Commercial lead | 48 hours | Written notice + report |
| Stakeholder complaint | PM | Sponsor | 24 hours | Phone + follow-up email |
