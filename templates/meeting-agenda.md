> Every meeting deserves an agenda. Circulate it at least 24 hours before the meeting (48 hours for steering committees). A meeting without an agenda is a conversation — valuable, but not a substitute for structured decision-making.

### Item Types

| Type | What is expected |
|---|---|
| **FYI** | Information only — no discussion required |
| **Discussion** | Input sought; no decision required at this meeting |
| **Decision** | A specific decision must be made and recorded |
| **Review** | Progress or status update; exception handling only |

## Ground Rules (standing)

*Customize as appropriate:*

- Start and end on time.
- One conversation at a time.
- Phones on silent.
- Decisions require a named decision-maker — not "the group."
- Actions require a named owner and a due date.
