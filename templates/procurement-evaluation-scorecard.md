> Use this scorecard to evaluate supplier proposals against pre-defined, published criteria. Complete individual scoresheets before a moderation meeting. Evaluation must remain independent until moderation. The commercial scores must be calculated separately and combined only after quality evaluation is complete (to prevent price influencing quality scoring).

## Scoring Scale

| Score | Label | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Non-compliant | No response, or response fails minimum requirements |
| 1 | Poor | Response provided but significant concerns; insufficient evidence |
| 2 | Acceptable | Meets the minimum standard; limited evidence or development |
| 3 | Good | Clearly meets the standard; adequate evidence provided |
| 4 | Excellent | Exceeds the standard; compelling evidence; innovation or added value |
